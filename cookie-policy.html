Cookie Policy

How Revenue Execution uses essential storage and optional analytics cookies.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Digital Sales Made Simple Ltd, an Irish-registered company trading as Revenue Execution. For questions about this policy, contact richard@revenueexecution.co.

2. Essential storage

The website stores your cookie preference in your browser so that we can remember whether you accepted or rejected analytics. This preference is necessary to respect your choice and does not track your activity across other websites.

3. Analytics cookies

If you choose Accept analytics, we load Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use Revenue Execution pages and resources. Google Analytics may then set cookies such as _ga and _ga_*. These analytics cookies are not loaded unless you give consent.

Analytics information may include pages viewed, referral source, browser and device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and selected website interactions. Revenue Execution uses this information to improve the website, resources and demand-generation activity.

4. Advertising

Revenue Execution does not use Google Analytics on this website for advertising personalisation. Google Signals and advertising personalisation signals are disabled in the website analytics configuration.

5. Your choice

You can accept or reject analytics when the cookie notice first appears. You can change your choice later by selecting Cookie settings in the website footer.

6. More information

For more information about how personal data is handled, see the Privacy Statement.
