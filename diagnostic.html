What it is

A clear answer to “what actually needs to change?”

This is not a generic health check. The diagnostic starts with a specific owner goal, target or business problem and works backwards to find the processes and behaviours that are producing the current result.

Best suited to

  • Revenue or growth below target
  • Profitability not improving with revenue
  • Pipeline or conversion problems
  • Weak forecast confidence or visibility
  • Owner dependency
  • Processes failing as the business scales

What you get

  • Clear definition of the business outcome and metric
  • Evidence-led view of the processes influencing it
  • Root causes and constraints, not just symptoms
  • Prioritised improvement actions
  • Clear decision on what to fix first
  • Option to stop, plan, or execute with Revenue Execution
How it works

Focused enough to buy. Deep enough to expose the real problem.

The exact evidence depends on the business issue, but the sequence remains the same.

01

Frame

Agree the owner goal, required outcome, relevant metric and what success would look like.

02

Investigate

Review data, process, customer or pipeline evidence and speak to the people closest to the work where needed.

03

Prioritise

Identify the highest-impact constraints and sequence the changes required to improve the metric.

The Revenue Execution diagnostic flow

From owner goal to measured result.

The diagnostic is not a general audit. It follows one primary outcome from the metric back into the processes, evidence and root causes producing the current result.

1

Owner Goal

What does the owner need the business to achieve?

2

Metric

What measurable result tells us whether the goal is being achieved?

3

Processes

Which 1–3 processes most directly influence that metric?

4

Evidence

What data, workflow or commercial evidence explains performance?

5

Gap / Root Cause

Where is performance breaking down, and what is actually causing it?

6

Improvement

What specific changes are most likely to improve the metric?

7

Execute

Implement the process, system, management or behaviour changes required.

8

Measure

Track the metric and confirm whether the intervention improved the result.

Diagnostic scope ruleOne primary owner outcome. One primary metric. Typically 1–3 important processes. Enough evidence to identify the real constraint and prioritise action — not a full-company transformation project.
