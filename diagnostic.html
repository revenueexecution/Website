Owner Business Performance Diagnostic

Find the real constraint before you invest in the fix.

A focused, evidence-led diagnostic for an owner who knows the business outcome they need to improve and wants clarity on what is actually preventing it.

The diagnostic logic

Start with your goal. Find what is creating the gap.

Define the outcome
Review the evidence
Identify the constraint
Prioritise action
What it gives you

A clear answer to “what actually needs to change?”

Start with the business outcome, target or problem. The diagnostic works backwards through the metrics, evidence and processes influencing it to identify the constraints that matter most.

Best suited to

  • Revenue or growth below target
  • Profitability not improving with revenue
  • Pipeline or conversion problems
  • Weak forecast confidence or visibility
  • Owner dependency
  • Processes failing as the business scales

What you get

  • Clear definition of the business outcome and metric
  • Evidence-led view of the processes influencing it
  • Root causes and constraints behind the current result
  • Prioritised improvement actions
  • Clear decision on what should change first
How it works

From business goal to prioritised action.

The exact evidence depends on the problem, but the sequence remains simple.

01

Frame

Agree the goal, required outcome, relevant metric and what success would look like.

02

Investigate

Review the data, process, customer or pipeline evidence and speak to the people closest to the work where useful.

03

Prioritise

Identify the highest-impact constraints and sequence the changes most likely to improve the metric.

Typical evidence

What I may look at.

The evidence is scoped around the problem. I focus on what can help explain the result and support a sound decision.

CommercialPipeline, conversion, pricing, retention, customer mix, deal progression, contracts and recurring revenue.
OperationalProcess steps, handoffs, ownership, manual work, bottlenecks, service delivery and onboarding.
ManagementForecasting, reporting, review cadence, accountability, decision rights and owner dependency.
PeopleRoles, capability, behaviours, incentives and where execution breaks down.
SystemsCRM, reporting, workflow and automation where they materially influence the result.
