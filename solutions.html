Solutions

Start with the business problem, not a pre-packaged service.

These are the issues I most often help owners diagnose and fix. Each can have several causes, so the first job is to identify what is actually driving the result.

Start with a diagnostic
01 — Growth

Growth has stalled or revenue targets are being missed.

The business has traction, but the next phase of growth is not happening consistently enough.

What may sit underneath it

  • Weak pipeline generation or poor pipeline quality
  • Low conversion or inconsistent deal progression
  • Positioning or value proposition no longer landing
  • Pricing, packaging or channel issues
  • Retention or expansion leakage
  • Capacity, process or management constraints
02 — Profitability

Profit isn’t keeping pace with revenue.

The business is busy and selling, but too little of that effort is turning into acceptable profit.

What may sit underneath it

  • Pricing and discount discipline
  • Customer or product mix
  • Cost-to-serve and delivery inefficiency
  • Commercial terms and scope leakage
  • Manual processes and duplicated work
  • Poor visibility into where margin is being lost
03 — Conversion

Pipeline exists, but it isn’t converting reliably.

There is activity and opportunity, but too much is stalling, slipping or disappearing.

What may sit underneath it

  • Qualification standards
  • Deal inspection and progression
  • Buyer alignment and value articulation
  • Sales process discipline
  • Manager coaching and intervention
  • Weak stage definitions or CRM hygiene masking reality
04 — Predictability

Forecasts and commercial visibility can’t be trusted.

You cannot see future performance clearly enough to plan, invest or intervene with confidence.

What may sit underneath it

  • Inconsistent forecasting methodology
  • Weak pipeline quality or stage confidence
  • No common management cadence
  • Fragmented reporting and data
  • Too much judgement and not enough evidence
  • Limited recurring or contracted revenue visibility
05 — Owner dependency

Too much still depends on the owner.

Customers, decisions, deals and operational knowledge keep coming back to you, limiting scale and creating risk.

What may sit underneath it

  • Unclear roles and decision rights
  • Weak delegation or management capability
  • Customer relationships held by one person
  • Processes that live in people’s heads
  • Insufficient reporting or management visibility
  • No operating rhythm that allows the business to run without constant owner intervention
06 — Scale

The business has outgrown the processes running it.

What worked when the company was smaller is now creating delays, inconsistency, unnecessary cost or poor visibility.

What may sit underneath it

  • Manual handoffs and duplicated work
  • Unclear process ownership
  • Inconsistent onboarding or service delivery
  • Weak management information
  • Systems that do not connect
  • Automation opportunities that have never been prioritised
One entry point

Don’t guess at the fix.

The same symptom can be produced by very different causes. The diagnostic identifies the constraint before money and time are committed to a solution.
