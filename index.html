FAQ

What owners usually want to know.

What kind of business is Revenue Execution for?

Owner-led businesses where growth, profitability, predictability, owner dependency or process constraints are materially affecting performance.

What does the diagnostic cost?

The Owner Business Performance Diagnostic starts from €2,500. Final scope depends on the outcome, evidence available and processes examined.

How long does it take?

Typically 1–2 weeks, depending on scope and access to information and people.

What happens after the diagnostic?

You can stop with the findings, ask me to build the implementation plan, or engage Revenue Execution to help execute the changes.

What if the problem is not sales?

That is why the work starts with diagnosis. The constraint may sit in pricing, retention, operations, management, process, data or execution.

What is preventing the business getting where you want it to go?

Start with the goal. We’ll work out whether there is a problem worth diagnosing.
