Why diagnosis matters

Performance is produced by a system, not one isolated function.

A growth problem might be pipeline, pricing, retention, proposition or execution. A profitability problem might sit in discounting, customer mix, process cost or operating discipline. The visible symptom is rarely the whole problem.
