How I help

Start with the business result you need to improve.

The website stays simple: Goal → Diagnose → Improve → Measure. The diagnosis determines what actually needs to change.

Grow Revenue

Find what is constraining growth — then fix it.

Growth problems are not always lead-generation problems. The constraint may sit in qualification, conversion, pricing, retention, expansion or execution.

What you want

Reach the next revenue target

For example, grow annual revenue from €4m to €5m.

What I diagnose

The processes controlling growth

  • Pipeline generation and quality
  • Conversion and win rate
  • Pricing and proposition
  • Retention and expansion
What we may change

The 1–3 constraints that matter most

  • Qualification standards
  • Sales process or messaging
  • Pricing / packaging
  • Account expansion process
How we measure it

Did growth improve?

  • Revenue growth
  • Win rate
  • Stage conversion
  • Expansion revenue
Example findingThe business has enough pipeline, but poor qualification and weak proposal conversion are suppressing growth. The answer is not “more leads”; it is better deal quality and execution.
Improve Profitability

Find where margin is leaking.

Profitability can be eroded by pricing, discounting, customer mix, delivery cost, manual work or commercial terms — even when revenue looks healthy.

What you want

Turn more revenue into profit

For example, improve gross margin from 28% to 35%.

What I diagnose

Where margin is being lost

  • Pricing and discounting
  • Customer / product mix
  • Cost-to-serve
  • Manual process cost
What we may change

The commercial or operational leakage

  • Pricing controls
  • Approval rules
  • Workflow redesign
  • Automation and reporting
How we measure it

Did margin improve?

  • Gross margin
  • Discount rate
  • Cost-to-serve
  • Revenue per FTE
Example findingRevenue is healthy, but uncontrolled discounting and an expensive manual delivery process are eroding margin. The improvement combines commercial discipline with process redesign.

Increase Predictability

Know what the business is likely to deliver before the period ends.

Predictability comes from evidence, common standards and management discipline — not better spreadsheet formatting.

What you want

A reliable view of future performance

For example, a dependable 90-day revenue forecast.

What I diagnose

Why the forecast cannot be trusted

  • Pipeline quality
  • Forecast methodology
  • CRM discipline
  • Management cadence
What we may change

The standards and visibility behind the forecast

  • Forecast rules
  • Evidence standards
  • Reporting
  • Review rhythm
How we measure it

Did confidence improve?

  • Forecast accuracy
  • Slippage
  • Pipeline coverage
  • Variance to actual
Example findingForecast calls are opinion-led because “commit” means something different to every seller. The improvement is a common evidence standard and a disciplined management cadence.
Reduce Owner Dependency

Build a business that does not need the owner in every decision.

Owner dependency usually persists because information, authority, customer ownership or process knowledge has not moved into the wider business.

What you want

Reduce day-to-day owner intervention

For example, operate for four weeks without constant escalation.

What I diagnose

Why work keeps coming back to you

  • Decision rights
  • Management visibility
  • Customer ownership
  • Role clarity
What we may change

The operating structure around the owner

  • Delegated authority
  • Management reporting
  • Account ownership
  • Operating cadence
How we measure it

Did dependency fall?

  • Owner interventions
  • Escalations
  • Decision speed
  • Management autonomy
Example findingManagers have responsibility but not enough information or authority to act, so routine decisions continue to escalate. The fix is visibility plus delegated decision rights.
Improve Process & Scale

Fix the processes the business has outgrown.

As companies grow, manual handoffs, duplicated work and unclear ownership can become expensive constraints on capacity and customer experience.

What you want

Increase speed, capacity and consistency

For example, reduce customer onboarding from 30 days to 10.

What I diagnose

Where the workflow is creating friction

  • Handoffs
  • Approvals
  • Manual touches
  • Rework and bottlenecks
What we may change

The workflow, ownership or automation

  • Process redesign
  • Clear ownership
  • System configuration
  • Automation
How we measure it

Did the process improve?

  • Cycle time
  • Error rate
  • Manual touches
  • Capacity / throughput
Example findingFour manual handoffs and duplicated data entry are causing delay and rework. The fix is a redesigned workflow with clearer ownership and selective automation.
